2. Scope

These terms and conditions govern the services offered by Devanture: design and development of custom websites, related options (back office, photography, video, AI-enhanced or AI-generated visuals, multilingual site, maintenance) and online presence setup (LinkedIn page, Google Maps listing, Facebook or X pages).

They are intended for professional clients (businesses, shops, independents). Placing an order implies full acceptance of these terms, which prevail over any other document from the client. Consumer clients, if any, keep the rights granted to them by law.

3. First call, project brief and quote

  • A free, no-commitment first call, by phone or video, to understand the client's business and needs.
  • If the client wishes to continue, a project brief is agreed together: it describes precisely what the site must contain and do, and serves as the reference for the whole project.
  • Devanture then issues a detailed quote stating the included services, the options selected, the price and the expected timeline. The quote is valid for 30 days from its date of issue.
  • The order becomes firm when the client signs the quote and pays the deposit described in section 5. Any request outside the project brief is subject to an additional quote.

4. Prices

The amounts shown on the website are indicative starting points. Only the signed quote is binding. Prices are in euros, excluding VAT. VAT not applicable, article 293 B of the French General Tax Code.

Express delivery (site ready in about one month instead of about three months at the standard pace) carries a surcharge of roughly 40% on the usual rate, stated in the quote, and remains subject to the provider's availability.

5. Payment

  • 30% deposit of the quoted amount upon signing, before any work begins.
  • Balance at delivery: the final invoice is issued when the site is delivered (put online or files handed over) and is payable on receipt. Final go-live and the full handover of access and files take place once the balance has been received.
  • Payment method: bank transfer, details stated on the invoice.
  • In accordance with articles L441-10 and D441-5 of the French Commercial Code, any late payment automatically incurs interest at three times the French statutory interest rate, plus a fixed recovery fee of 40 euros.

6. Timelines

The indicative timeline is about three months for a site that is genuinely ready to hand over, and about one month at the express pace. These periods run from receipt of the deposit and of the materials needed. They depend on the client's responsiveness (providing content, approving stages) and are given as an indication: a reasonable delay does not entitle the client to cancel or to compensation.

7. Client obligations

  • Provide the necessary content (text, images, logos, legal and practical information) within a reasonable time and warrant that they hold the required rights over it. The client is solely responsible for the content they supply and for its lawfulness.
  • Approve each stage within a reasonable time. If no answer is received within fifteen days of a request for approval, the stage is deemed approved.
  • Provide accurate information and report any change without delay.

8. Process, approvals and revisions

The site is built in stages, each submitted to the client for approval. Revision rounds are included within the limit set in the quote. Once a stage has been approved, any later change to it constitutes an additional request, priced separately.

9. Delivery, hosting and domain name

Delivery consists of putting the site online or handing over the files and access, as set out in the quote. The domain name and hosting are taken out in the client's name, who remains their holder; Devanture can assist with these steps. Domain and hosting fees are not included in the services unless the quote states otherwise.

10. Intellectual property

  • Once the price has been paid in full, the client becomes the owner of the delivered site: its specific design, its content and its pages, which they may use, modify and develop freely.
  • Devanture retains ownership of its tools, methods, templates, reusable components and generation engine, as well as its know-how, which are not transferred and may be reused for other projects. The client holds a perpetual right to use the components built into their site.
  • Content supplied by the client remains the client's property.
  • Unless the client objects in writing, Devanture may cite the project as a reference and show previews of it in its own communications.

11. Maintenance

Maintenance is optional and offered after delivery, as a monthly plan or on-request work. It is covered by a separate quote or agreement setting out its scope, duration and price. Without maintenance, Devanture is under no obligation to work on the site after delivery.

12. Services delivered with partners

Some options (photo shoots, video filming) may be carried out by independent partners coordinated by Devanture. The exact arrangements are stated in the quote.

13. Cancellation and termination

  • If the client cancels after signing the quote, the deposit is retained by Devanture. If the work completed exceeds the deposit, it is invoiced in proportion to progress.
  • If Devanture is unable to continue the service, any amounts paid that do not correspond to work completed are refunded.
  • In the event of a serious breach by either party not remedied within fifteen days of written notice, the other party may terminate the service.

  • Devanture is not liable for third-party services used by the site or in the client relationship (hosting provider, registrar, social platforms, booking, video-call or payment tools), nor for content supplied by the client.

Data provided by the client (name, contact details, information about their business) is used solely to respond to their request, prepare the quote and carry out the service.
