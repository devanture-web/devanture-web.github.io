AI agents

AI agents that work for you, under your control

An AI agent doesn't just answer a question: you give it a goal, it works through the steps on its own (searching, reading, sorting, writing) and hands you work that is ready to approve. I build them to measure, to give you back hours every week.

What is an agent?

A chatbot answers. An agent gets things done.

A regular assistant

You ask a question, it answers. Searching, cross-checking, formatting, doing it all again next week: everything else is on you.

  • One question, one answer
  • You do the steps
  • It forgets everything afterwards
An AI agent

You set a goal and some rules once. It plans, uses tools (web search, your documents, the access you give it) and delivers the result, every time it's needed.

  • One goal, one result
  • It works through the steps alone
  • It works every week
How it works

Three steps, and you stay in charge

  1. 01

    You set the goal

    For example: "every Monday, find me ten HR managers at manufacturing SMEs and draft an outreach message for each".

  2. 02

    The agent works

    It searches, compares, drops what doesn't fit your criteria and writes, following your tone and your rules.

  3. 03

    You approve

    You read, adjust if needed, then publish or send it yourself. Nothing goes out without you.

What your agents can do

The repetitive work, prepared for you

Your dashboard, with the Send button

Everything the agent prepares lands in a private page that belongs to you: an internal app, an online space reserved for your team, or a tool running on your own computer. You review, you adjust, you press Send. Nothing leaves without that click.

Find the right person for a role

You describe the profile you need (role, experience, industry): the agent finds possible candidates, explains why each one fits, and drafts the approach message for each. You choose who to contact.

Write your LinkedIn posts

Ideas and drafts for regular posts, drawn from your news, your projects or your market watch.

Publish your articles by itself

Once an article is approved, the agent puts it online on your website, your blog and your company LinkedIn page, at the date and time you choose, through each platform's official tools.

Follow up contacts by email

A follow-up sequence written in your tone, sent automatically by email, stopping the moment someone replies or unsubscribes.

Keep watch for you

Every week, a summary of what is moving in your industry: competitors, trends, new rules, with the links.

And, on the same principle: finding prospects from your criteria, drafting your outreach messages, writing your tweets and your in-depth articles.

Your safeguards

Automation without the risk

Nothing goes out without you

By default, the agent prepares and you approve. Only low-risk tasks you have signed off can be scheduled.

Platform rules respected

No bot scraping profiles or mass-sending messages on your behalf: that is exactly what gets LinkedIn accounts restricted or closed.

Access you control

The agent only uses what you open to it, and you can take it back at any time.

Work you can check

Every result is explained and comes with its sources, so you know where it came from.

Your questions

What people ask me about agents

What is the difference between an AI agent and ChatGPT?

ChatGPT answers when you write to it. An agent receives a goal and works through several steps on its own with tools (search, documents, calendar...), then hands you finished work to approve. An agent can itself run on ChatGPT, Claude or another AI model.

Does the agent publish or send things for me?

No, by default it prepares and you approve. For some low-risk tasks, such as scheduling a post you have approved, publishing can go through the platforms' official tools.

Can it prospect on LinkedIn on its own?

It prepares the profiles to contact and the messages, but you do the sending. LinkedIn's terms forbid bots that crawl the site or send messages automatically, and accounts caught doing so get restricted.

How much does an agent cost?

Like everything else, it's quoted based on what the agent has to do. There is usually a small AI usage cost on top, estimated upfront in the quote.

Do I need a website built by Devanture?

No, an agent can be built on its own. It's simply more useful connected to your site, for instance to prepare the articles that will appear there.
