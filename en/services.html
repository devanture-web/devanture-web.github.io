Custom design

Built around your business and your image, not a generic template reused a thousand times. You sign off on every step.

Mobile-first

Most of your visitors arrive from their phone: the site is designed to be just as clear there as on a computer.

Search engine optimization included

Multilingual if needed

Add-ons

Back office, photo, video, multilingual: options, never imposed

Each one is added independently of the others. We choose together what actually serves your business.

Back office

Edit your own text, photos and prices yourself, without touching any code and without needing me for every change.

Professional photography

An on-site photo shoot by a photographer I know and trust, or by one near you: visuals that genuinely look like you, not stock photos.

Video

An introduction video generated with AI, or filmed on site by a videographer I know and trust, or one near you, whichever look you prefer.

AI-enhanced or AI-generated photos

I enhance your existing photos (light, background, sharpness), or create new ones if you don't have visuals yet.

Verified multilingual site

Translation, AI proofreading, and if you'd like, review by a native speaker of each language before launch.

Maintenance

I keep your site up to date and respond to your requests, on a monthly plan or on request, whichever you prefer.

Print-ready QR codes

For your tables, shop window or flyers. They lead straight to your website, with no subscription or middleman, and never change.

Online presence

Google, LinkedIn, Facebook: one consistent image wherever people look for you

Your website is the hub. I create or take over your pages on the platforms that matter for your business, with the same words, the same visuals and a link to the site everywhere: your customers recognise you, and so does Google.

New: AI agents

Agents that handle the repetitive work

Finding prospects, writing your posts, tweets and articles, keeping watch on your industry: an AI agent prepares, you approve.

Understand AI agents
  1. 01You set the goalFor example: "every Monday, find me ten HR managers at manufacturing SMEs and draft an outreach message for each".
  2. 02The agent worksIt searches, compares, drops what doesn't fit your criteria and writes, following your tone and your rules.
  3. 03You approveYou read, adjust if needed, then publish or send it yourself. Nothing goes out without you.
