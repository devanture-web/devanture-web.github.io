Frequently asked questions

What businesses and shops ask me most

A question that isn't here? The first call is free, and that's exactly what it's for.

How much does a website cost for a business or a shop?

A showcase site starts at €1,200 excl. VAT. Beyond that, the amount depends on the number of pages and the options chosen: a clear, detailed quote is provided after a free first call, before anything begins.

Which languages can we work in?

English or French: I'm bilingual, for calls, messages and the whole project. I can also get by in Spanish for a call, if you prefer.

How long does it take to build a website?

Three months maximum at the standard pace, and one month maximum at the express pace, often less when your content is ready. Custom sites often take six months or more when content and approvals drag on: here the deadline is written into the quote. Express is available as an option (surcharge applies): that pace becomes almost my sole priority during that period.

I don't have professional photos yet, is that a problem?

No: a professional photo shoot can be arranged on site, with a photographer I know and trust or one near you, and visuals can also be created or enhanced with AI if you prefer that option.

I don't want to manage my site myself, is that possible?

Yes. The back office (to edit your own text and photos) is optional, not a requirement. Without it, maintenance can be handled directly, on a monthly plan or on request.

Is SEO (Search Engine Optimization) really included?

Yes, from the basic tier onward: site structure, copy and load speed are all planned for search engines from the start, not bolted on afterwards.

How does payment work?

In two parts: a 30% deposit when you sign off on the quote, before any work starts, then the balance at delivery, once the site is live.

What is an AI agent, and what can it do for me?

An AI agent receives a goal and works through the steps on its own: searching, sorting, writing. It can, for example, find prospects that match your criteria, draft your LinkedIn posts, tweets or site articles, or keep watch on your industry. It prepares, you approve before anything is sent.

Can you build an app, not just a website?

Yes: custom apps for your team (internal tools) or for your customers, available through a simple link or published on the App Store and Google Play. As with websites, every project gets a free quote.
