What makes the price vary

A quote is not a number pulled out of a hat

Here is exactly what I look at to price a project. The longer the bar, the more that line weighs on the final amount.

The number of pages A single home page or fifteen pages of content is not the same job.
What the site has to do Present your business, or sell online, take bookings, configure a product.
The number of languages Every added language is proofread, not just machine translated.
The content to produce You provide texts and photos, or I have them produced (photo, video, retouching).
The pace 3 months maximum as standard. Express means 1 month maximum: I block my time for you, roughly 40% more.
What happens next You take over on your own, or I stay available for maintenance.
The quote is free The first call and the quote that follows cost nothing and commit you to nothing.
Payment in two parts 30% deposit when you sign the quote, the balance at delivery.
The price does not move Once the quote is signed, the amount is firm. Anything added along the way goes into an amendment that you approve before I start it.
