Your questions

What people ask me most

Can I change my menu myself?

Yes, with the back office option: you update dishes, prices and hours from a phone or a computer, without going through me.

Can the site take bookings?

Yes. It can plug into the booking tool you already use, or simply highlight your phone number and email if you'd rather keep control.

Can the QR code stop working?

Not mine: it leads straight to your website, with no middleman service or subscription. Many free "dynamic" QR codes stop when the trial ends; this one stays valid as long as your site exists, and never changes.

Isn't a PDF menu enough?

A PDF reads badly on a phone and Google understands it less well than a page. A text menu helps you show up when someone searches for a dish or a type of food.
